Fueling app screens

iPhone 17 screens (402 × 874 pt), including the quick-add sheet, for a 70 kg cyclist on Tuesday 27 Oct, in light and then dark, followed by more states in light mode. The numbers are the v1 example day in DESIGN.md (guideline bands only), so they differ from the wireframes on purpose. Every phone screen scrolls.

Frames whose caption carries After beta are outside the beta: Goal weight, Caffeine, the Week overview, food search and the barcode scanner.

The system is locked in DESIGN.md; the audit of the wireframes is in the Hallmark audit.
